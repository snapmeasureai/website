FAQs

Apparel sizing questions

How do body measurements help reduce size-related returns?

Many size-related returns start with a shopper guessing their size from a chart. A two-photo scan replaces the guess with the shopper's own measurements, which your size logic compares with each garment's chart. Shoppers who sit between two sizes can be told so — instead of ordering both and sending one back.

How accurate is it?

Average accuracy is 97% in everyday clothing, across 18 standard measurements, tested on people photographed in varied clothing and compared with 3D body scans. For context, reliability studies of trained measurers using a tape report waist measurement errors of 1.3–6.5 cm, and the body itself changes through the day. Results are best in fitted clothing, with arms slightly away from the body. The best test is your own: try the free live demo, or run a pilot that compares scans with your size charts.

Do shoppers need to undress or download an app?

No. Fitted clothing gives the best results, but everyday clothes such as jeans and T-shirts work too; only coats and bulky layers come off. The scan runs inside your own site or app on any smartphone, with nothing to install.

Can made-to-measure brands use the scan for patterns?

Yes. Define the measurements your patterns use — any circumference or length between points on the body — and every scan returns that set, plus an OBJ 3D body model for pattern and CAD software. Customers can be measured at home instead of booking a fitting.

Does this replace our size recommendation engine?

It can feed one. The scan returns body measurements, not a size: your own size logic, or the recommendation provider you already use, turns them into a size for each garment. Optional confidence ranges show when a shopper is close to a size boundary.

What do you store about our customers?

Nothing that identifies them. Photos are processed in memory and deleted once the result is returned, and a scan needs no name or email. The measurements go to your system, where you can save them to the customer's profile and reuse them across your catalogue.

Can the experience carry our brand?

Yes. The capture flow and the optional 3D viewer are white-label, or your team can build its own interface and call the API directly — see the developer docs.
