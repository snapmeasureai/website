The Services estimate body measurements, a 3D body model and related figures from photos. These are estimates. Their accuracy depends on things like:

  • lighting, camera angle and distance;
  • your pose;
  • clothing (fitted everyday clothes give the best results); and
  • the height you enter.

Results can differ from scan to scan and from tape-measure results. Figures such as the body-fat estimate and waist-to-height ratio come from standard formulas applied to the estimated measurements. They are not body-composition measurements.
